How to Vote Online

A quick step-by-step guide for electronic voting.

Didn't receive the voting email? That usually means your email address is not registered for electronic voting. Please contact the office to get enrolled so you can vote online.

Tip: check spam/junk folders first. If you still don't see it, the office can confirm your registration status.

Quick steps:

  1. Open the electronic voting email from the Association.
  2. Click the voting link and follow the prompts.
  3. Submit your vote and keep the confirmation for your records.
