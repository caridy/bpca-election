Tracy Harrington

Director, joined board 2025

Why I'm running: My name is Tracy Harrington. I joined the Board in 2025 to help ensure that as Brickell Place completes its major structural and recertification work, the focus shifts to livability, aesthetics, and the quality of shared spaces. I've been an owner at Brickell Place for 15 years, and I care deeply about this community. My background in design, project coordination, and strategic communications gives me a unique perspective on how to improve the owner experience during a complex construction period and beyond.

What I bring: I bring a combination of design expertise, project coordination experience, and communications skills that are directly relevant to the challenges Brickell Place faces:

• Professional experience in interior design and project management for residential and commercial properties.
• Strategic communications and public affairs background, with a focus on clarity, transparency, and stakeholder engagement.
• A focus on quality-of-life improvements and how shared spaces can better serve the community.
• A commitment to ensuring that as construction phases wind down, the building transitions from "work zone" to "home."

What to expect: My priorities as a Board member include:

• Advocate for aesthetics, cleanup, and livability as major construction phases conclude.
• Support clear, consistent communication with owners about timelines, decisions, and next steps.
• Help ensure that capital projects are completed with attention to detail and long-term value.
• Focus on shared-space improvements that enhance daily life for owners and residents.

The goal is to help Brickell Place feel like a home again, while preserving the progress already made.

Professional background: Senior roles in public affairs, strategic communications, and media relations for government, corporate, and nonprofit organizations. Founder, Tracy Harrington Interiors / Ruxton Home Managers, providing design and project coordination services for residential and commercial properties. B.A. Political Science.
