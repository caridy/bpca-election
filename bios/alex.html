Alexander Magrisso, Esq.

Director, joined board 2023

Why I'm running: My name is Alex Magrisso, and I am a licensed attorney in the State of Florida currently serving on the Brickell Place Board of Directors. I am seeking re-election to continue supporting the Board through ongoing legal, regulatory, and compliance matters as the community completes its most complex phase of work. Having served on the Board for the past 3 years, I'm deeply familiar with Brickell Place's recertification efforts, capital projects, and regulatory issues.

What I bring: I bring formal legal training combined with direct Board experience, allowing me to contribute immediately and effectively. My background includes:

• Practicing law with a focus on municipal law, real estate, land use, zoning, and regulatory compliance.
• Three years on the HOA Board, with active involvement in recertification, compliance, and legal matters.
• Working closely with building, planning, zoning, and code-enforcement processes.
• Advising leadership on legal risk, compliance obligations, and regulatory interpretation.
• Coordinating with city, county, and state agencies on complex regulatory issues.

This combination of legal expertise and institutional knowledge is particularly important as Brickell Place completes structural work and navigates ongoing regulatory requirements.

What to expect: As a Board member, my focus is to:

• Provide legal and regulatory perspective to support sound, well-informed decisions.
• Help anticipate and address compliance issues before they escalate.
• Support constructive, professional engagement with municipal and regulatory authorities.
• Maintain clear boundaries between governance and day-to-day operations.
