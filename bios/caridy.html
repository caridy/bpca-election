Caridy Patiño

Board President since 2021

Why I'm running: My name is Caridy Patiño. I currently serve as Board President and am seeking re-election to help Brickell Place complete the work already underway and transition from recovery to long-term stability and quality of life. I have been an owner here for more than a decade, and both of my children were born in this community. Since joining the Board, my commitment has been simple: steady leadership, open communication, and seeing the job through.

What we've done (results and stewardship): Since 2021, the Board has navigated one of the most complex periods in Brickell Place history—an aging waterfront property facing overdue structural and regulatory requirements, major restoration work, and a financial reset.

Key areas of progress include:

• Advanced major structural and compliance work, including progress through the 40/50-year recertification requirements and ongoing restoration efforts.
• Stabilized long-term financial planning by building stronger guardrails, modern processes, and committee-driven oversight to avoid crisis budgeting.
• Professionalized governance by creating committees, improving access to information for owners who want to engage, and maintaining clear boundaries between Board governance and day-to-day operations.

What to expect next (the job isn't finished): The next term is about finishing strong and restoring normalcy—without losing control of the progress made:

• Complete remaining recertification and capital work with disciplined oversight and predictable planning.
• Shift from "construction zone" to "home": prioritize cleanup, aesthetics, and quality-of-life improvements.
• Improve owner experience with management communications by strengthening tracking, follow-through, and clarity on request channels—without the Board micromanaging staff.
• Protect property values with stability: thoughtful planning, reserves strategy, and careful decision-making.

Professional background: Computer Scientist B.S., Principal Software Architect at Salesforce.com, Inc. Former Yahoo! Inc. Alumni. Permanent member of international standards organizations including ECMA International and the W3C.
