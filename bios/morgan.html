Morgan Sirlin

Treasurer, joined board 2025

Why I'm running: My name is Morgan Sirlin. I currently serve as Treasurer of the Board, and I'm running to help ensure Brickell Place completes its recertification and capital projects with strong financial discipline, clear planning, and a focus on long-term value. My wife and I moved to Brickell Place in 2021, and our first child was born here. Like many owners, this building is not just an investment for us — it's our home.

What I've focused on (execution and oversight): I joined the Board as a working professional with a background in real estate development, finance, and building operations, experience that directly applies to the challenges facing an old condominium undergoing major work.

Key areas of responsibility include:

• Leading coordination of the 40/50-year recertification efforts, working closely with engineers, contractors, architects, management, and legal advisors.
• Oversight of complex budgets and capital planning, including insurance proceeds, assessments, and long-term funding strategies.
• Strengthening financial clarity and predictability, helping ensure owners understand how major projects are funded and how financial decisions are made.
• Supporting major safety and beautification initiatives, including pool deck and perimeter improvements, as the building moves out of crisis mode.

What to expect next: The work ahead is about closing out major projects and transitioning to a more stable operating rhythm:

• Complete remaining recertification and restoration milestones with disciplined financial oversight.
• Maintain predictable planning to avoid unnecessary surprises for owners.
• Improve how financial information is explained and contextualized for complex construction phases.
• Support the shift from emergency-driven decisions to long-term, value-focused stewardship.

Professional background: Real estate development, finance, and operations professional with day-to-day responsibility for large budgets, capital projects, insurance, and construction oversight.
