Jessica Bosworth, CPA

Finance Committee

Jessica Bosworth is a Certified Public Accountant with extensive experience in financial reporting, auditing, and corporate governance. She began her career at PwC, where she spent five years as an auditor working with complex organizations and developing a strong foundation in controls, compliance, and financial accuracy.

Jessica has spent more than 13 years at Norwegian Cruise Line, where she currently serves as Senior Director of SEC Reporting and Technical Accounting. In this role, she oversees public company reporting, technical accounting matters, and complex financial disclosures, working closely with executive leadership, auditors, and attorneys. Her background includes deep experience with budgets, contracts, compensation structures, and high-stakes financial decision-making.

Within the community, Jessica serves on the Finance Committee and is Treasurer of Miami Mensa, reflecting her commitment to responsible stewardship and clear financial oversight. She brings a thoughtful, detail-oriented approach to governance and is focused on protecting long-term value through transparency, accountability, and sound financial management.
