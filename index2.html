2025: The Delivery Engine

This was the year where planning became visible execution across the community, while keeping governance open and financially disciplined.

System modernization scaled up: critical infrastructure work accelerated across valves, life-safety, mechanical rooms, pumps, and generators.

Governance and financial control held steady: open meetings, direct owner access, and disciplined controls kept execution moving without disorder.
