Collections Promise Kept

Five years of disciplined execution brought delinquency from crisis levels to industry-leading performance

The Starting Point (2021)

When the current board took office in 2021, HOA delinquency was approximately 4%, while the original special assessment delinquency exceeded 10%. The commitment was clear: bring total delinquency below 2.5% through consistent enforcement, improved systems, and fair but firm collection practices.

Understanding the 2023 Spike

In 2023, the board approved a second special assessment to fund critical recertification work. The temporary spike in delinquency reflects the natural adjustment period as owners were notified, payment systems were updated, and electronic payment infrastructure was implemented. This initial increase was expected and quickly reversed through systematic outreach and improved collection processes.

The Progress

What This Means

Industry Context: Delinquency below 2% is generally considered strong performance for condominiums in South Florida. Below 1.5% is exceptional, particularly for a building undergoing major construction and managing multiple special assessments.

Financial Impact: Lower delinquency means more predictable cash flow, stronger reserves, better borrowing terms, and reduced legal costs. It also signals to potential buyers that the building is well-managed and financially stable.

How It Was Done: Modernized payment systems, consistent board oversight, professional management follow-through, clear owner communication, fair payment plans when appropriate, and firm enforcement when necessary.

Both HOA and Special Assessment

Current delinquency rates are below 1.5% for both regular HOA dues and special assessment obligations, demonstrating that owners are meeting their commitments even during a high-cost construction period.
