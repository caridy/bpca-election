Roof System Replacement

A multi-million dollar rebuild of both tower roof systems delivered improved protection, reduced insurance costs, and long-term structural reliability.

What We Inherited

The existing roof systems on both towers had reached end-of-life, creating ongoing leak risks, maintenance burdens, and insurance exposure. Years of deferred replacement had left the building vulnerable to weather events and escalating repair costs.

Both Towers Complete roof system replacement
Multi-Million Total project investment
Insurance ↓ Reduced premiums for 2025-2026 cycle

What Was Delivered

Full system replacement: Both tower roofs were completely rebuilt with modern materials and installation standards, eliminating legacy leak points and structural vulnerabilities.

Blended funding strategy: The project was funded through a combination of reserve funds, special assessment proceeds, and a portion of the IRMA insurance award, demonstrating strategic use of available capital sources.

Insurance cost reduction: The upgraded roof systems contributed to measurably lower insurance premiums for the 2025-2026 policy cycle, providing ongoing financial relief to owners.

Minimal disruption execution: The work was completed while residents remained in occupancy, with phased scheduling to limit noise and access impacts.

Why This Matters

Roof replacement is one of the highest-cost, highest-impact capital projects a condominium can undertake. Completing this work in 2025 eliminated a major liability exposure, improved building protection against future weather events, and delivered immediate insurance savings. The blended funding approach also demonstrated disciplined capital planning by leveraging multiple sources rather than creating unnecessary assessment burden.

This project represents the kind of long-term stewardship that protects property values and reduces future emergency spending.
