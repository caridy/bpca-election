Window Program Mobilization

Legal recovery was converted into an active, owner-facing execution program at large scale.

What Was Resolved

The board inherited an unresolved IRMA-related dispute and worked to close the loop with funds dedicated to envelope replacement.

~$4M Recovery scale used to support replacement scope
100+ Core units in original replacement scope
300+ Units registered for program participation

What Changed in 2025

From legal file to field work: the effort moved from dispute handling into procurement, enrollment, and mobilization planning.

Owner participation: the program expanded beyond the initial minimum scope as additional owners elected to participate.

Execution readiness: technical and operational prerequisites were addressed to support rollout.

How Owners Can Verify

  • Settlement and board communication records describing award use.
  • Program registration lists and rollout communications to owners.
  • Construction and operations updates tied to installation scheduling.

Why This Matters

Window replacement directly affects building envelope performance, storm resilience, and long-term property value. Mobilizing this scope at community scale is a major execution milestone, not just a planning statement.
