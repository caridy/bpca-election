Life-Safety Modernization

A multi-year, special-assessment-funded rebuild turned a damaged fire system into a code-aligned, operational life-safety platform.

What We Inherited

After IRMA-related water damage, the main fire alarm panel was compromised. A prior panel swap had already triggered full code-upgrade requirements, because replacing a major fire system component requires bringing the complete system to current code.

2023 SA Primary funding source for full life-safety rebuild
~40% Garage sprinkler piping replaced
Tower A: Final Building B + townhouses in final-inspection closeout

What Was Delivered

Full-system upgrade path: the board coordinated with the fire department, engineer, and contractor to rebuild the system to code across both towers and the townhouses.

Hardened critical equipment: fire panels were relocated to a more secure area, and the annunciator was moved to the front gate for practical emergency response.

Fire suppression reliability work: approximately 40% of garage sprinkler piping was replaced, along with key pumps and supporting infrastructure.

Emergency communications compliance: code-required BDA antennas were installed so firefighters can maintain radio coverage throughout the property.

Access readiness: traffic patterns were adjusted to improve fire truck entry and maneuverability during emergency response.

Current Status

The upgraded system is operational. Final inspection is complete for Tower A, with final inspections pending for Building B and townhouses to close permits.

How Owners Can Verify

  • Permit files and final-inspection records by tower/townhouse scope.
  • Engineer and contractor submittals for fire alarm, BDA, and suppression upgrades.
  • Meeting records documenting phasing, funding, and closeout status.

Why This Matters

This was a surgical, multi-year execution effort done while residents continued daily life with minimal disruption. It reduced high-liability risk, improved emergency readiness, and brought core life-safety infrastructure to a materially stronger baseline.
