Core Systems Modernization

A large share of neglected infrastructure was repaired or replaced to restore reliability and control.

What Was Addressed

The modernization effort covered multiple layers of critical operations: mechanical rooms, water-line controls, pumps, generators, access systems, and property-wide monitoring.

10k → <2k Access registrations cleaned and normalized
Property-wide Camera coverage extended across common areas
Both Towers Valve and mechanical reliability scope advanced

Execution Approach

Risk-first sequencing: priorities were set around system fragility, safety implications, and operational continuity.

In-house capability build: maintenance and security teams were expanded to improve speed, accountability, and quality control.

Governance oversight: committee and board review ensured work stayed aligned with compliance and budget priorities.

How Owners Can Verify

  • Work orders, procurement records, and mechanical-room before/after logs.
  • Security and access-control transition records.
  • Committee reports tracking system modernization milestones.

Why This Matters

System reliability is the backbone of resident experience. This was a foundational modernization cycle, not isolated maintenance, and it reduced day-to-day operational risk across the community.
