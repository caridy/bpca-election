Security + Access Upgraded

A multi-year transition delivered in-house security operations, broader coverage, and modern access control across a complex multi-entry property.

What We Inherited

The community relied on a high-cost external guard agency for 24/7 coverage, an outdated access platform, and a registration database that had drifted far from reality over time.

6+ Acres Property coverage expanded with camera system upgrades
30+ Doors Modernized access points plus multiple vehicular gates
10k+ → ~2k Fob/vehicle registrations normalized to real active users

What Was Delivered

In-house security department: operations transitioned from external agency staffing to an internal team, with hiring, training, and performance stabilization over multiple years.

Operational resilience under pressure: the transition was executed while dozens of contractors were active in common areas and towers, during concurrent structural and life-safety projects.

Property-wide camera expansion: cameras were installed in every hallway and across nearly the entire property footprint.

Privacy-first implementation: upgrades were delivered without facial recognition, biometric profiling, or other privacy-invasive features, consistent with resident requests.

Access platform replacement: the legacy system was replaced with Gallagher and ButterflyMX across a large number of entry points and gates.

Clean credential governance: registrations were reduced from legacy inflation levels to a controlled, current-state dataset.

Short-term rental control: mobile credentials and stricter issuance controls make illegal short-term rental activity significantly harder by preventing anonymous key/fob handoffs and requiring approved credential workflows tied to screening requirements.

How Owners Can Verify

  • Board records for security and access transition decisions.
  • Procurement/implementation records for camera, Gallagher, and ButterflyMX rollout.
  • Credential-audit snapshots showing pre-cleanup vs current registration counts.
  • Resident communications and implementation notices during cutover periods.

Why This Matters

The result is a modern control environment built for a complex, high-access property, delivered with minimal day-to-day disruption for residents. In practical terms, the current platform is at a level comparable to, and in some areas stronger than, newer buildings because it had to solve a harder access topology while also tightening illegal short-term-rental risk.
